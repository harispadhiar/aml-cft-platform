# 🛡️ National AML/CFT Intelligence & Regulatory Platform

Interactive demonstration of the Pakistan Anti-Money Laundering & Counter Financing of Terrorism compliance platform.

### Capabilities:
- **Watchman Screening:** Screening against NACTA 4th Schedule, UN Sanctions 1267/1988, and PEP databases.
- **Deterministic Risk Scoring:** Rule-based deterministic risk assessment (Structuring, High-Risk Jurisdictions, Velocity Spikes).
- **RAG Regulatory Citations:** SBP AML/CFT/CPF Regulations 2020 & FATF 40 Recommendations.
- **AI SAR/STR Narrative Generator:** Mistral-7B QLoRA fine-tuned on FMU e-filing 5W+H guidelines.
- **Human-in-the-Loop (HITL) Adjudication:** Stagegate transition recording.
- **Cryptographic Audit Log:** SHA-256 block-chained immutable audit log with live tamper verification.
- **FMU JSON e-Filing Export:** Compliant STR/SAR payloads for submission to the Financial Monitoring Unit.
